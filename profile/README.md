# SmartUrbanity

## Advancing 15-Minute Cities through Collaborative and Smart Urban Solutions

![SmartUrbanity Banner](https://img.shields.io/badge/Project-SmartUrbanity-green) ![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Active-success) ![Duration](https://img.shields.io/badge/Duration-36_months-blue) ![Funding](https://img.shields.io/badge/EU_Funding-€3M-orange)

### 🎯 What is SmartUrbanity?

Imagine living in a city where everything you need - work, shopping, healthcare, schools, and parks - is just a **15-minute walk or bike ride away**. That's the vision of SmartUrbanity!

We're building digital tools that help cities become more liveable, sustainable, and inclusive by putting citizens at the heart of urban planning.

---

## 🚀 Our Mission

SmartUrbanity transforms how cities understand and respond to their residents' needs. We're creating a digital bridge between **citizens and city planners** to build better urban spaces together.

### The Problem We're Solving
- 🚗 Cities are too car-dependent
- 🏢 Essential services are often far away
- 👥 Citizens rarely have a say in urban planning
- 📊 Cities lack real-time data about mobility needs

### Our Solution: A Digital Toolkit
```
    Citizens → Share experiences → Citizen App
                    ↓
              Data Platform
                    ↓
             Analysis Tools
                    ↓
             City Planners
                    ↓
            Better decisions
                    ↓
             15-Minute City
                    ↓
              Benefits → Citizens
```

---

## 🛠️ Our Digital Tools

### 1. 📱 **Citizen Engagement App**
A mobile app where you can:
- Report urban problems (unsafe streets, missing sidewalks)
- Track your daily trips
- Suggest improvements
- Earn rewards for participation

### 2. 🗺️ **Accessibility Analysis Platform**
An interactive web platform showing:
- Heat maps of accessibility issues
- Community-voted priorities
- Real-time urban mobility data
- Customizable filters for different user needs

### 3. 🎯 **Decision Support System**
Tools for policymakers to:
- Simulate policy impacts
- Analyze mobility patterns
- Evaluate interventions
- Get community feedback

### 4. 🎮 **Gamification System**
Making participation fun with:
- 🟢 **Green Points** for eco-friendly trips
- 🟡 **Yellow Points** for community contributions
- 🏆 Badges and achievements
- 🎁 Real rewards (cultural events, sports activities)

## 💡 How It Works

1. **Citizens use the app** to report issues and track trips
2. **Data is analyzed** to understand mobility patterns
3. **Community votes** on priorities
4. **City planners** use insights to make better decisions
5. **Cities become** more liveable 15-minute communities

### The Process Flow
```
📱 Citizen Input
    ↓
📊 Data Collection & Analysis
    ↓
🗳️ Community Prioritization
    ↓
🎯 Evidence-Based Planning
    ↓
🏙️ Better Cities for All
```

## 🔬 Innovation & Technology

### Machine Learning & AI
- Mode detection algorithms for transport analysis
- Predictive models for urban mobility patterns
- AI-powered policy impact assessment

### Agent-Based Modeling
- MATSim for large-scale mobility scenarios
- mobiTopp for detailed travel demand modeling
- Real-time simulation of policy interventions

### Hybrid Artificial Intelligence
- Combining physics-based and data-driven approaches
- Real-time diagnosis and decision-making
- Climate-aware urban planning
